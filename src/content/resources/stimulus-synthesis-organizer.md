## After the table

Answer in complete sentences.

1. Which two sources are in the sharpest disagreement, and about what?
2. Which source would be dangerous to quote without the others beside it? Why?
3. What can I claim now that I could not claim from any single source?

## Stems that force synthesis

Use these only if they are true. Delete the stem once the sentence can stand alone.

- Read together, these sources suggest that…
- Source A establishes …, but Source B limits that claim by showing…
- The packet agrees about … and does not agree about…
- My question has to change because…

A stimulus packet is not a menu of quotations. It is a set of perspectives you have to put in a relationship before you are entitled to a thesis.
