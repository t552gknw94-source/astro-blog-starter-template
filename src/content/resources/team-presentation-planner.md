## The through-line

Sketch the presentation in order. Each speaker writes the sentence they will say that connects their section to the one before it.

1. Open the problem and the question.
2. Perspective:
3. Perspective:
4. Perspective:
5. Synthesis: what the perspectives, taken together, support.
6. Implication or limit, and a close that does not introduce a new topic.

## Rehearsal checks

- [ ] Someone who missed the research can follow the argument without reading the reports.
- [ ] Slides hold evidence and names, not a script of every word.
- [ ] We have practiced who will take which kind of defense question.
- [ ] We can point to the report behind any claim on a slide.
- [ ] The solution or conclusion does not ignore a perspective we included earlier.

Time the run. Then cut one slide. Teams almost always learn more from the cut than from the extra example.
