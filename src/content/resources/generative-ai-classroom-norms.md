These norms are the class agreement. They sit beside the [AI research protocol](/resources/ai-research-protocol/), which tells students how to do the work. Read both before a major writing task.

## Authorship

The student is the author of claims, structure, and commentary. A tool can support a named step. It cannot be the person who decided what the paper thinks. If you could not explain a paragraph to a classmate, it is not ready to turn in, no matter how it was produced.

## Disclosure

If a tool was used, say so in a short process note: the tool’s job, what you kept, and what you checked. Disclosure is a normal part of academic honesty, like naming a peer who helped you find a source. Hidden use is the problem, not the note.

## Privacy

Do not paste another student’s draft, a classmate’s personal story, family information, or anything you would not want stored outside the school. Seminar stories stay in the room. When a tool is allowed, use your own writing and public sources.

## What helps, and what flattens

A tool can help when it widens a search, translates a dense sentence you then verify, or shows you that your own paragraph is hard to follow.

A tool flattens the work when it hands you a thesis, a structure you did not choose, or a voice that sounds like nobody in particular. Formal is allowed. Absent is not. If the draft no longer sounds like a decision you made, revise until it does.

## For families

You can help by asking what the question is, what your student believes so far, and which source changed their mind. You hurt the learning when you, or a tool you operate, supply the argument. The teacher would rather see an honest, unfinished line of reasoning than a polished paper the student cannot defend.

## For teachers adapting this page

State the rule on the assignment itself, not only in a syllabus from August. Tell students which phase of the protocol is in play. Grade the thinking they can show, including the process note. A norm that is never looked at during a conference is a poster.

If school or district policy is stricter than this page, the school policy wins. Put that sentence on your copy so students are not caught between two adults.
